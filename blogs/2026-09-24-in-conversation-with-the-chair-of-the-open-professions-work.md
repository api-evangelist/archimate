By Ash Patel – CDMP – Certified Copywriter (CMP) – CDMA, Content Marketing Manager, The Open Group Recently, we reached out to Vicky Rose of Kyndryl Consult to discuss her role as the Chair of The Open Group Governing Board Open Professions Work Group. Vicky is a Principal Enterprise Architect at Kyndryl with over 20 years’ experience as an architect and is an Open Professions Certified Distinguished Architect. She is based in the UK, responsible for Kyndryl UK & Ireland Architecture Governance.
