By Ash Patel, Marketing Specialist, The Open Group. The Open Group hosted a virtual event on Enterprise Architecture (EA) for Sustainability July 25 - 27, 2023, assembling leading industry and global subject matter experts. The event looked at Enterprise Architecture (EA) helping organizations identify sustainability goals, along with focusing on the TOGAF® User Group, and The Open Group India Awards for Innovation and Excellence.
