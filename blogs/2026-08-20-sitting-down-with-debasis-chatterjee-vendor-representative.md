By Ash Patel – CDMP – Certified Copywriter (CMP), Content Marketing Manager, The Open Group Recently we reached out to Debasis Chatterjee of Katalyst Data Management to discuss his role as a vendor representative to The Open Group OSDU® Management Committee. Debasis is a seasoned Information Management professional with over 44 years of experience in handling Geoscience data (“Oil and Gas”, “Mining” and other domains). He works for Katalyst Data Management as “Director – Research and Analytics” and is involved in product developments and client projects.
